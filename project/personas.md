# Personas

## Labourer with a phone wanting to start a business in his field

- He's a labourer, working in construction, and he's looking to start a business in his field
- Confined to being employeed and doing repetitive work, low upward mobility without changing jobs
- Decent position, but stretched with his family
- Has a phone, and is looking to try something else
- Wants to commit to something, no money to spend on it
- Has periods of downtime, but low energy, so needs it to be engaging, interesting, enriching
- Wants to be able to do it part-time, and scale up as he goes, small steps but with a clear path to the end goal
- Small wins each time be engages with the project

## Student wanting to start a business in his area

- He's a student, looking to start a business in his area
- Has a phone and a laptop, and is looking to try something else
- Has education, wants to learn and also provide help to those in need around him to be apart of the business 
- Both engaged in the project, as well as a connector to find others to engage in the project

## Teacher wanting to provide help to pupils who are lagging or lost

- He's a teacher, connected to hundreds of students per year
- Various different classes, teaching methods, but not enough time to provide help to each student individually
- Wants to be able to provide help to students who are lagging or lost, as well as check in with them from time to time to provide assistance and guidance
- Able to refer people in, as well as provide assistance to the business side if students want to start a business with him, or trial it in the school or university
