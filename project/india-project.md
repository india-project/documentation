# India Project Principles

- Meeting people where they're at
- Recommending no risky investments
- Preservation first, growth second
- Experimentation and learning
- Realtime language translation (remove barriers to access)
- A serious engineering focus on "as cheap as possible" maximising effect of dollars on access once we have a feedback loop of engaged userss
- Net positive goal: we're able to receive value from the project, and in turn, reinvest it into the project to create a virtuous cycle
- We don't know what people need, and also can't forsee what people are struggling with, so it's most important to get in and start collecting information and building things, whatever they are - then learn, and try new things from the new spaces we're able to access
- Safety-first, no "all in" approaches, no debt
- Small compounding steps

Summary:

> Practical tools and safe paths for people to build stability and optionality, starting where they are
